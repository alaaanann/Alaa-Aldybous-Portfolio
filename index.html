About me

I'm a senior Electrical & Computer Engineering student at Effat University, working where hardware meets intelligence: embedded systems, edge AI, power electronics and signal processing.

This summer I joined the CMS HGCAL team at CERN, testing detector readout electronics and building the hardware catalogue the team now uses. Today I'm designing a time-domain reflectometry leak-detection system at Saudi Sustainability Technologies, and I chair the IEEE Robotics & Automation Society chapter at my university.

Beyond tech, I draw, watercolor, read fantasy, watch K-dramas and practice Taekwondo. To me, engineering is just another form of art.
